The problem

Every engineering project runs on documents that have to agree. Checking that they do is manual.

A package runs to hundreds of documents, each citing others by number, revision, standard and value. When one changes, the rest have to be checked by people reading line by line — and what slips through is found on site, as rework.

The product, today

It works, and anyone can try it.

The validation core reads PDF and Word, extracts document numbers, revisions, standards, quantities and rated values, and cross-checks them across the whole set. The public demo runs those checks in the visitor’s browser on their own files.

  • Missing documents — cited, but not in the package
  • Outdated revisions — Rev 2 cited where Rev 3 is current
  • Standards cited in two editions
  • Quantities that differ between a list and a drawing
  • Design values that differ between documents

How it grows

From one partner’s documents to a platform.

  1. Stage 1 · Co-development

    A launch partner

    Early access, a version tailored to their documents and standards, and the choice of shared IP or exclusive use. BravoDoc gains real packages to learn from.

  2. Stage 2 · Pilots

    Real data, measured

    Cross-document validation and custom rules, piloted on live projects, with the time saved measured against the manual check.

  3. Stage 3 · Platform

    Enterprise SaaS

    A full platform with integrations into document systems and models that learn from each project, for wider rollout.

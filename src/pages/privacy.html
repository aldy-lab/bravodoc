Privacy

What this site does with your data.

Last updated 14 October 2026

In short

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Files you check in the demo are read in your browser and never sent anywhere.
  • What you write in a form reaches Bravo Integrated Solutions, and is used only to answer you.

Who is responsible

Bravo Integrated Solutions runs this site and BravoDoc. For anything about your data, write to .

The demo

When you drop or choose files, the page reads them with JavaScript inside your browser: PDF with pdf.js, Word with mammoth, both served from this site. The text, the findings and the report exist only in that browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored or logged. The downloaded report is created on your device.

Forms

The pilot and investor forms collect your name, company, work email, your interest and anything you choose to write. Depending on how the site is set up, sending the form either opens your own email app with the message filled in, or sends it to a form service that forwards it to our mailbox. We use it to reply and to prepare a proposal, keep it for as long as the conversation is useful, and do not sell or share it. The legal basis is your request (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the page and protect the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to its use, and complain to your data protection authority.

        FAQ

        Questions engineering teams ask first.

        Does BravoDoc replace the engineer’s review?

        No. It reads every document against every other one and lists what does not agree, with the line it found it on. An engineer decides what each finding means. The time it saves is the searching and comparing, not the judgement.

        Which file formats does it read?

        PDF and Word today, which is what the demo on this page uses. Scanned PDFs and drawings (DWG, DXF) need OCR, which is in development and part of the pilot.

        Which standards does it check against?

        Today it checks that every standard is cited in the same edition across the package — IEC, EN, ISO, DIN and others, by number. Checking content against your own standards and limits, as custom rules, is the next step on the roadmap.

        Where do our documents go?

        In the demo, nowhere: files are read in your browser. In a pilot, where it runs and who can see the documents is agreed with you before any file is shared. It is built cloud-ready for AWS and Azure.

        What does a pilot involve?

        You choose a real document package. We tune the checks to your numbering and standards, run them, and go through the findings with your engineers. You see what it catches on your own work before any wider rollout.

        What does it cost?

        A pilot is scoped with you, depending on the package and the rules it needs. Ask for a proposal through the form below.
